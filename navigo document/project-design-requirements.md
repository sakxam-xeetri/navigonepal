# PROJECT OVERVIEW

* Project Name: Navigo Nepal

* One Line Description:
  A premium youth empowerment and educational impact platform connecting students, schools, mentors, innovators, and opportunities across Nepal.

* Primary Goal:
  Establish Navigo Nepal as Nepal's leading youth-led educational organization while increasing student participation, volunteer recruitment, partnerships, and national impact visibility.
