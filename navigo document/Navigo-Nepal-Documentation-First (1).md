## **1. About Navigo Nepal** 

Navigo Nepal is a youth-led, non-profit organization dedicated to empowering junior high school students to confidently navigate their futures by bridging the gap between academic learning and real-world skills. We aim to equip students with the essential knowledge, skills, and opportunities needed to become capable learners, responsible citizens, and global leaders. Through interactive sessions, hands-on experiences, and nationwide outreach, we provide exposure to scholarships, career paths, and community leadership, encouraging students to explore their potential beyond the classroom. Our programs, which include informative seminars, competitions, and club management workshops, emphasize teamwork, personal development, and innovative thinking. At Navigo Nepal, we are committed to transforming the educational landscape by fostering an environment where students can discover their passions, set meaningful goals, and contribute positively to their communities, guided by our mantra: learn, lead, and lift 

## **1.1 Problem Statement** 

In Nepal, many students are confined to a narrow educational experience focused solely on academic achievements. This approach often leaves students unprepared for the practical challenges they will face in their careers and personal lives. The lack of exposure to different opportunities and insufficient guidance on post-secondary options create a significant gap between what students learn in school and what they need to succeed in the real world. 

## **1.2 The Role of Navigo Nepal** 

Navigo Nepal was established to address these gaps by providing students with the exposure, guidance, and skills they need to navigate their educational and career paths successfully. Our programs are designed to help students discover their interests, develop essential life skills, and gain clarity on their future goals. 

## **1.3 Our Mission and Vision** 

Our mission is to empower students across Nepal by transforming their educational experience from a narrow focus on academics to a more holistic approach that includes personal development, leadership, and problem-solving skills. Our vision is a future where every student in Nepal has the knowledge and tools to make informed decisions about their education and career. 

## **1.4 What Makes Us Unique?** 

Navigo Nepal stands out because we don’t just advocate for change; we actively create it. Our sessions are interactive and engaging, using games, discussions, and real-world scenarios to make learning fun and impactful. We also focus on practical experiences, such as camps and workshops, to ensure that students gain the hands-on skills they need to succeed. ## **2. The “What's Next?” Campaign** 

## **2.1 Problem Statement** 

Many students in Nepal complete their secondary education without a clear understanding of what their next steps should be. This uncertainty can lead to poor decision-making regarding further education and career choices. The lack of guidance and exposure to different academic and career paths is a significant barrier to student success. 

## **2.2 Objectives** 

The primary objective of the "What's Next?" campaign is to provide students with the knowledge, resources, and support they need to make informed decisions about their future. We aim to: 

- Empower students by helping them understand their options after secondary school. 

- Provide detailed information on choosing the right college and course based on individual interests and strengths. 

- Equip students with the skills needed to establish and run successful student clubs, fostering leadership and teamwork. 

## **2.3 Campaign Overview** 

The "What's Next?" campaign is a comprehensive program that includes counseling sessions, workshops, and hands-on activities designed to help students explore their 

academic and career options. We begin by assessing students’ needs and interests through surveys and tests. Then, we conduct interactive sessions that cover everything from college selection to skill development for extracurricular activities. Our goal is to help students see beyond the classroom and recognize the broader opportunities available to them. 

## **2.4. Session Structure and Methodology** 

## **2.4.1 Session Overview** 

Navigo Nepal’s sessions are designed to be dynamic and engaging, tailored to meet the specific needs of students. Each session is carefully structured to maximize student participation and learning, ensuring that the material is relevant and impactful. 

## **2.4.2 Session Presentation Preparation** 

## **Preliminary Survey** 

A Qualitative survey was conducted on more than 100 students of different districts and schools to know about the major problems in learning that they have been facing in their school. The survey was done in online format through zoom online call and we made 

sure that every student’s ideas were recorded. We analyzed their various opinions and solution measures and came up with a comprehensive project to encompass every area. 

Similarly, a quantitative survey was also conducted on students of a specific school to learn about the situation of extra-curricular activities in their school. The survey was in paper format and random sampling method was employed for this purpose. Through the survey, we gained a clear picture of what we have to provide exactly while doing our sessions in the future.

## **5. Challenges and Recommendations** 

## **5.1 Challenges Faced** 

Implementing educational programs in a diverse country like Nepal comes with its challenges. However, Navigo Nepal has consistently found ways to overcome these challenges through innovation and adaptability.

Through our experience, we have learned valuable lessons about what works and what doesn’t in educational interventions. Based on our experiences, we recommend that future initiatives focus on increasing student involvement, expanding the use of technology in education, and building stronger partnerships with schools and local communities. ## **Leadership Training at Gyanodaya and Padmodaya Schools, Kathmandu** 

## **The Beginning: A Request for Change** 

After the successful implementation of our leadership projects at Shree Chandra Jyoti School in Nuwakot, and What's Next: Dhading, we received an unexpected but exciting request. The teachers from Gyanodaya School, impressed by our previous work, reached out to us with a special mission. They wanted us to conduct a personality development session specifically for their Scout students. It wasn’t just about leadership skills—they wanted their students to see how personal growth could go hand in hand with academic progress. 

We saw this as an incredible opportunity. We didn’t want to follow the traditional approach of lecturing students on leadership. Instead, our vision was to make learning a fun and engaging process. We believed that the best way to help these students was to provide real-time experiences that would show them how to not only grow as individuals but also take their studies to the next level. 

## **The Experience: Learning Through Play** 

When the day arrived, we stepped into Gyanodaya School with a carefully crafted plan. Rather than beginning with a typical introduction, we immediately set the tone by involving the students in interactive games. The Balloon Game and the “How Many F’s” game were our way of breaking the ice while embedding 

leadership lessons. These games weren’t just for fun—they were designed to give students a firsthand experience of the core skills we wanted to instill in them: teamwork, communication, and critical thinking. 

By using these activities, the students didn’t just learn; they applied what they had learned in real-time. For us, this was the key to the session’s success—making the lessons not just memorable but transformative. As the students participated, we saw them challenge themselves, communicate better with one another, and develop leadership skills that they would carry with them beyond the classroom.

## **Project with AISEC** 

Navigo Nepal got an incredible opportunity to collaborate with AISEC, a global organization on their program on Sustainable Development Goals (SDG). Members of Navigo Nepal were distributed across different schools of Kathmandu alongside other volunteers of AISEC. SDG 13- Climate Action was chosen among other SDGs to make young students aware about the climate change that has been happening because of mankind and how they can reduce it through their efforts in home and school. 

Before going to school for the session, two orientation sessions were given by AISEC heads to event goers on how to carry out the program. Different roles were assigned to each organizer on each class of a school. Most of the schools didn’t have proper infrastructure to incorporate PowerPoint presentations, so we had to carry out our session through other ways like group discussions, activities and games. At first, we introduced the concepts of SDG and United Nation, climate change and how organizations like AISEC are addressing the issue of climate change worldwide. We made the topic of climate change relatable to students’ daily lives and how it can be reduced through small steps from home and school. We organized fun games like drawing and also awarded the top performing students. 

Main challenge during this program on almost every school was to control the students as they were noisy and uninterested at first. But as the session progressed with more fun activities and crucial topics, student started to actively engage with us and asked questions and gave various solutions as well. Students shared creative ideas like making baskets from waste plastic, reusing waste hair and many others out of the box examples.

## **Expanding Nation wide** 

The central idea behind the formation of Navigo Nepal is to provide essential exposure to underprivileged students of the country. For that, we have travelled to numerous districts and met with a lot of students coming from every caste, ethnicity and background. Our mission to connect with communities across Nepal has led us to various villages, including Nuwakot, Dhading, Butwal, Rasuwa, Rupandehi, and Banke. 

We believe that we are still missing a lot of such students scattered all over the country in rural areas of Nepal and sometimes even in urban schools. Some places don’t even have the proper infrastructure to provide normal education to students where the concept of ECAs and clubs are rarely understood and implemented. We aim to reach out to these areas and expand our programs nationwide. 

For meeting this purpose, navigo Nepal has successfully organized many sessions in different districts of Nepal. Up until now, we have reached to 24 districts and we are determined to take this number to more than 35 districts. ## **Sustainability** 

One of the most important aspects of success of any organization is its sustainability. At this stage, Navigo Nepal has been making success after success in every school we go. To continue this progress in the future as well and to sustain, many plans and strategies have been formulated like: 

A) Volunteer recruitment: We, as an organization which aims to reach to every corner of the country, need to have a lot of individuals working on it. For that, we have formulated a way to recruit volunteers which involves application process and interviews. We look for individuals who have passion for doing something good for their community and who can actively contribute to our common goals. 

B) Concept of district ambassador- Navigers: Navigo Nepal identifies a student from schools of each district as someone who has exceptional leadership and teamwork capabilities to represent Navigo Nepal for their district. They will be called ambassadors or Navigers as we like to call it and will provide us information about various clubs established by us there and condition of ECAs in their schools. That way, it’ll be easier for us to understand what a school is in need of so that we can make tailored content for them.
