## **1. What till now?** 

Firstly, we started Navigo Nepal when we felt about what we were deprived of in our school days. Navigo is the latin name of Navigate which is our main motto: to navigate future with hands-on learning. 

We wanted to let students find "What's Next?" which was our first project; 3e realized that there is a huge education gap between grade 10 and 11, we wanted to provide the bridge so that they can wisely choose what they want to study after their basic schooling. 

To know more about this, we first took a need based assessment test with about 30 students to know them and their goals. We found out most of them were not clear about their goals, or why were they even studying. The school had provided them with nothing but the knowledge to complete their SEE, and that’s it. Hence, we figured out that they have to be suggested about what was about to come. Initially, our plan was to give them all the information about what they can study in +2 and share our experience on how to make that experience enjoyable, but we later realized, through the assessment test that everyone had different skills. Many people wanted to pursue things that were not given by any course. Hence we chose to not only counsel them, but to make them a problem-solver. We wanted to end the conventionality our education system holds and give them knowledge about what they can choose in such a way that they themselves could figure out what they would choose, and what they want to become in the future. 

To kick start our project, first we went to Shree Chandra Jyoti School of Nuwakot to provide exactly that. Our campaign expanded to help them understand how they can make a positive impact on their community. Guiding students on how they can form clubs inside their school and engage in extracurricular activities, all while sparking a problem solving attitude in them, our campaign achieved a major success. 

We then went on to our next project. We stayed at Brahmeshwor Gurukul, firstly just as a guest, but when we realized that despite them having all the computer resources they needed, no one was using them. Then our next project of connecting Tech to Veda began. We gave them training on how to use computers, we taught them the wonders of Artificial Intelligence and how can we implement those things on real life. We are still on the process of researching on this project, about the implementation to connect Veda with tech more intuitively. Our major objective is to connect other students with spirituality, which have been with us from centuries. 

Our third project started when we were approached by Gyanodaya School, by seeing the potential of us through above two projects. We wanted to conduct the same project as we did in Shri Chandra Jyoti School, but due to the large volume of students, the school administration suggested us to do something for the scout. So we prepared a Problem-solving and Leadership Training presentation. We implemented many interactive games in our sessions which would teach them something deeper about their career and future. Those scout students were by far the most enthusiastic and best audience we had till then. After getting lot of positive feedback from them, we conducted a similar project at Padmodaya School (Namuna School of Kathmandu) as well. 

Our most recent project was to collaborate (informally) with the BaBa program of Kathmandu Metro in helping generate awareness among the people about our environment on World Environment Day. We, along with other organizations creatively raised awareness for the serious issue of climate change. 

While all these projects seem vastly different from one another, we tried to develop ProblemSolving attitude in the audience. We developed our presentations in such a way that they could figure out the solutions by themselves. This is what we want to do and pursue. 

We make learning a fun process; we are employing interactive sessions, game, group discussions, debate between students, solo presentations, field trips to provide them exposure. We simply want to change the existing petrol system happening in the country.

## **How do we run our program?** 

- 1) We run surveys throughout the country to know what problems students are facing, what are their core needs to flourish their overall academic experience. 

- 2) We take a need based assement test in the school one or two days before our main session. 

- 3) We take the inspiration for our content based on our existing research, surveys, and need based assement test and generate our content. 

- 4) Then after going to the school, we first conduct a baseline survey also known as diagnostic test to know their current ability. 

- 5) We conduct our main sessions. 

- 6) We then take end line survey at the end our session and compare the impact we had with the results from our diagnostic test. 

- 7) We validate our impact through teachers and students by taking interviews. 

- 8) We then prepare our final draft for the second session to be handled in the same school.

## **What’s Next? Campaign** 

We plan to continue and expand our "What's Next?" campaign, providing comprehensive counseling to students on their post-secondary options. We anticipate conducting this program in designated colleges from July 22, Friday. 

The main objective of this program is to provide guidance and support to students as they navigate their educational paths after completing their 12th grade. Our program aims to: 

- **Empower Students** : We will help students understand the various options available to them after 12th grade, including different fields and colleges. Our goal is to equip them with the knowledge to make informed decisions about their future education. 

- **College and Course Selection** : We will present detailed information on how students can choose the best college and course that aligns with their interests, strengths, and career aspirations. This will include insights into admission processes, scholarship opportunities, and factors to consider when making their choices. 

- **Skill Development for Student Clubs** : In addition to academic guidance, we will provide students with the skills needed to establish and run successful student clubs within their school. This includes leadership, organization, communication, and teamwork skills, which are essential for personal development and fostering a vibrant school community. 

We have felt first hand and still see that students of grade 9 and 10 develop a passion for things like sports, community engagement and research, but as they transition themselves to +2 phase, the burden of books, exams and continuous fear of getting left behind makes student monotonous on studies and are compelled to pause their passion. We want to revive that passion in them and provide them a platform where they can showcase their talent other that studies. We are determined to help those students in making use of their talent and making their school studies fun. We incorporate ideas like VR and hands on experience to the various concepts they learn in their classrooms. In short, we aim to free the minds of students from the walls of classrooms and let them see the vast universe of vast opportunities and spark a enthusiasm to change the world in a better way.
