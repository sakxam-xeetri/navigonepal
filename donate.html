How You Can Help

Choose How to Donate

Select the type of donation that works best for you. Every contribution directly supports students and schools in need.

Donate Money

Financial contributions help us organize workshops, provide learning materials, cover transportation costs, and support school programs across Nepal.

  • Rs. 500 can provide stationery for 5 students
  • Rs. 2,000 can fund a career guidance session
  • Rs. 10,000 can sponsor a full school club launch

Donate Books

Your used textbooks, novels, and reference materials can become valuable resources for students who lack access to educational books.

  • Textbooks (SEE, +2, Bachelor's level)
  • Reference guides and prep materials
  • Children's books and storybooks
  • English and Nepali dictionaries

Donate Goods & Products

Stationery, school supplies, and educational products help students who cannot afford basic learning materials.

  • Notebooks, pens, pencils, erasers
  • Bags, pencil cases, geometry boxes
  • Art supplies and craft materials
  • Used laptops or tablets for learning

Impact

How Your Donation Helps

01

Materials Reach Students

Donated books, stationery, and supplies are distributed directly to students in partner schools across Nepal, starting with communities most in need.

02

Programs Are Launched

Financial donations fund career guidance workshops, leadership training sessions, school club formations, and environmental campaigns in rural schools.

03

Communities Grow

Every donation creates a ripple effect — students who receive support become mentors, leaders, and changemakers in their own communities.

A youth-led movement bridging the transition to higher studies and equipping students with real-world skills.
