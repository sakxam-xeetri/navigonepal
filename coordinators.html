Our Network

Nationwide Reach,
Local Impact

Navigo Nepal's coordinator network spans across 7 districts, with district-level ambassadors ensuring programs are tailored to local needs. Each coordinator manages a team of volunteers who deliver hands-on sessions directly in schools.

From urban centers in Kathmandu to rural communities in Nuwakot and Dhading, our coordinators are the bridge between Navigo's mission and the students who need it most.
